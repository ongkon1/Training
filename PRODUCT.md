# Training Monitoring

## Product Overview

Training Monitoring is a training administration and performance-monitoring platform for trainers and students. It brings student records, training results, spoken assessments, feedback, and performance reporting into one shared system, giving education teams a consistent place to manage assessment activity and giving students direct access to their own progress.

The product addresses the fragmentation and manual effort involved in maintaining student details, recording marks, calculating grades, reviewing performance, and following up on completed trainings. Trainers can manage student accounts, maintain written or manually scored results, monitor class performance, and use configurable AI-assisted evaluation to produce feedback. Students can review their results, see strengths and areas that need improvement, and take voice-based trainings through the platform.

A central workflow supports spoken trainings conducted by web call. After a call ends, the platform receives the training record, matches it to the appropriate student, retains its processing status, and can automatically generate a score, grade, summary, and feedback. The resulting assessment appears alongside the student's other training results, so trainers and students do not need separate systems for spoken and conventional assessments.

The platform is intended primarily for trainers or training administrators who oversee students and assessments, and for students who take trainings and review their performance. Role-specific workspaces keep administrative controls with trainers while limiting each student to their own profile, trainings, and results.

## Features

### Role-Based Access

* Provide separate, secure workspaces for trainers and students.
* Direct users to the appropriate dashboard and tools after sign-in.
* Restrict each student to their own results, voice trainings, and profile information.
* Keep class and roll-number information under trainer control while allowing students to maintain permitted personal details and passwords.

### Trainer Dashboard and Performance Monitoring

* View total student numbers and the overall average result from a central dashboard.
* Compare student growth and result trends across selectable 7-, 30-, or 90-day periods.
* See how students are distributed across excellent, good, average, and needs-improvement performance bands.
* Filter performance summaries and recent student activity by class.
* Open a recent student's full profile and result history directly from the dashboard.

### Student Management

* Create and maintain student accounts with contact, class, roll-number, date-of-birth, and address information.
* Search the student register by name, email address, or roll number.
* View each student's profile together with their complete result history.
* Update or remove student records as administrative needs change.
* Start recording a result directly from an individual student's profile.

### Training Result Management

* Record results by student, training, subject, date, full marks, marks obtained, and optional trainer remarks.
* Calculate percentages and consistent letter grades automatically from the recorded marks.
* Edit or remove results when corrections are required.
* Filter the result register by student and subject.
* Keep manually entered and voice-training results together in the same student record.

### AI-Assisted Evaluation and Feedback

* Generate individualized written feedback from a student's result details and trainer remarks.
* Re-evaluate a result when updated guidance or a revised assessment is needed.
* Configure the evaluation instructions used for a trainer's students, supporting consistent feedback aligned with the trainer's expectations.
* Show whether AI evaluation is ready for use and clearly identify results that already include generated feedback.
* Make completed AI feedback available to both the trainer and the relevant student.

### Voice Training Workflow

* Allow students to start and complete spoken trainings through an embedded web-call experience.
* Track each student's recent voice trainings and show whether processing is pending, evaluated, unmatched, or failed.
* Receive completed call records and associate them with the correct student using registered call and phone information.
* Automatically turn a completed spoken assessment into a scored result with marks, percentage, grade, and written feedback.
* Produce a concise call summary when transcript content is available.
* Add completed voice-training results to the same result history used for other assessments.

### Voice Training Review and Recovery

* Give trainers a consolidated view of spoken assessments and their current processing status.
* Filter voice-test records by status to focus on completed, pending, unmatched, or failed items.
* Alert trainers when a call cannot be matched to a student and indicate the phone information that needs attention.
* Review the student, score, grade, AI feedback, call summary, timing, and call outcome for an individual voice test.
* Retry an incomplete evaluation after correcting a matching, configuration, or processing issue.
* Adjust a generated result when trainer review identifies a necessary correction.

### Student Performance Experience

* Give students an at-a-glance view of their total results and average performance.
* Highlight their strongest subject result and the result that most needs improvement.
* Present recent results with marks, percentages, and grades.
* Provide a complete, paginated result history with detailed views for each assessment.
* Show trainer remarks and AI-generated feedback alongside the associated result.
* Let students update permitted profile and contact information without changing trainer-managed academic identifiers.

### Integration Monitoring and Process Control

* Record incoming voice-training callback activity, including accepted and rejected requests, for operational review.
* Show the time, source, response status, and outcome of each incoming request.
* Retain malformed or unmatched submissions for investigation instead of silently discarding them.
* Prevent repeated callbacks for the same call from creating duplicate assessment records.
* Protect voice-training submissions with a configurable shared credential and show trainers whether that protection is active.
* Allow authorized trainers to clear the operational request log when it is no longer needed.

## Benefits

### Reduce Manual Assessment Work

Automatic percentage and grade calculation removes repetitive arithmetic from result entry. For spoken trainings, automated matching, scoring, summarization, and feedback generation further reduce the work required to convert a completed assessment into a usable student record.

### Improve Processing Speed

Voice assessments can move from a completed call to a recorded result without requiring a trainer to transcribe and score every response manually. Students receive a structured result through the same workflow used for their other trainings, shortening the time between assessment and feedback.

### Centralize Student and Assessment Information

Student profiles, conventional results, spoken assessments, trainer remarks, and AI feedback are retained in one place. This gives trainers a more complete view of each learner and gives students a single destination for reviewing their performance.

### Increase Performance Visibility

Trainer dashboards convert individual results into useful indicators, trends, and performance bands. Class filters and recent-student views help trainers identify patterns and learners who may need attention without reviewing every result individually.

### Improve Feedback Consistency

Trainers can establish reusable evaluation guidance and apply it across relevant assessments. This supports a more consistent tone and assessment approach while retaining the trainer's ability to re-evaluate, edit results, and add their own remarks.

### Support Student Improvement

Students can see their average, strongest performance, area needing work, detailed marks, grades, remarks, and generated feedback. This makes progress easier to understand and provides clearer direction for future study.

### Strengthen Operational Control

Status tracking, unmatched-call alerts, retry tools, and incoming-request records make voice-training problems visible and recoverable. Trainers can identify where processing stopped and take targeted action rather than losing an assessment or recreating it from scratch.

### Reduce Errors and Duplicate Records

Consistent grade rules, role-based controls, student ownership checks, call matching safeguards, and duplicate-callback handling reduce common administrative and attribution errors. These controls help keep assessment records accurate and associated with the correct learner.

### Scale Assessment Activity

Search, filtering, pagination, automated evaluation, and summarized dashboards allow trainers to manage growing numbers of students and results with less effort per assessment. The combined written and spoken result history also avoids parallel administrative processes as new assessment formats are introduced.

## Business Outcome

Training Monitoring turns student administration and assessment activity into a controlled, visible, and reusable workflow. By combining student records, result management, performance reporting, voice-based trainings, and AI-assisted feedback, it reduces manual effort, accelerates assessment processing, improves consistency, and gives trainers and students clearer insight into performance. The overall result is a more scalable assessment operation with better traceability, fewer avoidable errors, and more timely support for student improvement.
